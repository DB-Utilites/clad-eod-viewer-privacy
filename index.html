CLAD EOD Viewer - Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

Overview

CLAD EOD Viewer is a browser extension designed to help authorized CLAD users review job-related end-of-day reports and prepare editable Word documents.

Information Accessed

The extension accesses information available to the user through their authenticated CLAD session. This may include job numbers, project details, technician names, crew information, work descriptions, comments, and photographs.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to display reports, identify possible follow-up items, summarize work activity, and generate editable Word documents at the user's request.

Data Processing and Storage

In version 1.0, report processing and Word document generation take place locally in the user's browser. The extension does not upload retrieved report information to a developer-operated server or analytics service.

Report information is held in the extension's active browser page during use. The extension does not currently persist retrieved reports using browser extension storage. Exported documents are downloaded to the user's device, where they are managed by the user.

Data Sharing

The extension does not sell user information or transmit retrieved reports to advertising or analytics providers. Users may choose to share documents they export. Such sharing is controlled by the user and subject to their organization's applicable policies.

Authentication and Permissions

The extension uses the user's existing CLAD session. It does not request or store CLAD passwords. Its website access is restricted to app.withclad.com.

Support

The extension provides an optional email link for reporting issues. Sending an email is a separate, user-initiated action, and the user controls its contents.

Policy Changes

This policy may be updated if the extension's features or data-handling practices change.

Contact

Questions about this extension or its privacy practices can be directed to dbrown@phoenix-fiber.com.
